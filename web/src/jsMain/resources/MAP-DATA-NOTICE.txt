Map data notice

The town in this page is traced from City of Vancouver open data. The map data built into vancouver-month.js
(VancouverMapData: the grid of land, water, park and shore, the shoreline, the park's outline, the rail line, the line
in Burrard Inlet, the city blocks and small parks) is derived from these datasets of the City of Vancouver Open Data
Portal, https://opendata.vancouver.ca, fetched on 2026-10-31:

  Shoreline 2002
  Parks - polygon representation
  Rapid transit lines
  City boundary
  Local area boundary
  Block outlines
