Map data notice

The town in this page is traced from City of Vancouver open data. The map data built into vancouver-month.js
(VancouverMapData: the grid of land, water, park and shore, the shoreline, the park's outline, the rail line, the line
in Burrard Inlet, the city blocks and small parks) is derived from these datasets of the City of Vancouver Open Data
Portal, https://opendata.vancouver.ca, fetched on 2026-10-31:

  Shoreline 2002
  Parks - polygon representation
  Rapid transit lines
  City boundary
  Local area boundary
  Block outlines

Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Vancouver.
https://opendata.vancouver.ca/pages/licence/

The map data is offered under that licence (version 1.0), not under the MIT licence of the game's code. The City of
Vancouver does not endorse this game.

The wide map (WideMapData, east through East Vancouver into Burnaby), wherever the page uses it, is also derived from
the City of Vancouver's Rapid transit stations and Public streets, and from these datasets of City of Burnaby Open
Data, https://data.burnaby.ca, credit City of Burnaby, fetched on 2026-10-31 (Vancouver time):

  Burnaby Boundary
  District Lot
  Legal Block
  Park Inventory
  SkyTrain
  SkyTrain Station
  Street
  Community Plan Area Boundaries
